Create a room or join a friend's code. Both players need an internet connection.
How to play
  • Online: One player creates a room and shares the 6-digit code. The other joins. The host runs the match; bots remain active. Online mode is a beta and works best when this file is hosted on an HTTPS website.
  • Move: joystick (phone) or WASD / arrow keys. E = use, R = report, Q = kill.
  • Crewmate: follow the yellow arrow to your tasks. Find a body? Hit REPORT. You can also call one emergency meeting at the red button in the Cafeteria.
  • In meetings, read what everyone says, accuse someone, then vote. Throw out the impostor or finish every task to win.
  • Special roles: Engineers can vent, Scientists check Vitals, Sheriffs can shoot (miss and you're out!), Detectives scan players. Shapeshifters disguise as others, Phantoms turn invisible. The bots have roles too, so watch out for the Sheriff!
  • Sabotage: As Impostor, use SABOTAGE to cycle Lights, Communications, and Reactor. During a Reactor meltdown, crewmates must reach the Reactor and press FIX before the timer ends.
    • \n
  • Impostor: KILL crewmates when nobody's watching. Anyone nearby will see you! Lie in meetings and blame others. Win when only one crewmate is left.
  • You can only see what's close to you, so watch out in dark corridors…
